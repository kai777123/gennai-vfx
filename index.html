FAQ

Godot 4.4 to 4.7

The Godot packs need Godot 4.4 or newer and are tested on 4.4.1 and 4.7.2. Ice & Frost was also tested on 4.5.2, 4.6.3. Versions in between should work too.

Web exports and Compatibility

They're tested on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility, and Web exports use Compatibility. The itch pages have browser demos you can try first.

License

You can use a pack in commercial or non-commercial games, apps and videos. It's royalty-free and needs no credit, and exported builds that contain it are fine. You can't resell or redistribute the pack itself. LICENSE.txt in the download has the full text.

Free and paid versions

A free version is a small slice of its pack, enough to try the look and the setup. The paid pack has the whole preset list and all the demo scenes. The itch page of each free version says what's in it.

Unity support

These ship with a Unity package: Pixel Art Laser Beam & Lightning VFX (ARCBIT); Pixel Art Explosion & Smoke VFX. The other packs are Godot 4 only.
