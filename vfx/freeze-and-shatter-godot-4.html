Freeze and shatter any mesh in Godot 4

Updated 2026-11-16. Tested on Godot 4.4.1, 4.5.2, 4.6.3 and 4.7.2, Forward+ and Compatibility.

To freeze a mesh in Godot 4, put a GlaciluxFreeze3D under the MeshInstance3D and call freeze() with the hit position. Frost spreads over the surface from that point. After that you have three ways out. thaw() melts the ice with drips and leaves a wet sheen. shatter() breaks it into shards of the mesh itself. reset() puts everything back exactly as it was. It also follows an animated character once you point the node at the AnimationPlayer. This is GLACILUX, which has 49 ice and frost presets for Godot 4.4 and newer and uses no textures. The code is below, and the demo runs in the page when you press the button.

The code

$Crate/Freeze.freeze(hit.position)                 # frost spreads from the hit point
$Crate/Freeze.thaw()                              # melts, drips, leaves a wet sheen
$Crate/Freeze.shatter(hit.position, 1.4)          # breaks into shards of itself
$Crate/Freeze.reset()                             # back to exactly how it started

Freeze is a GlaciluxFreeze3D under the MeshInstance3D. Give it a shatter_delay and it breaks by itself that many seconds after it's fully frozen.

Try it in your browser

It loads the pack's own demo, about 8 MB the first time, and it opens on its first scene, “Freeze anything”. Left and right arrow keys, or the on-screen buttons, move to other scenes. A desktop browser works best; the first start can take up to a minute while the shaders compile.
